Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here are honest answers regarding our AC maintenance, jet washing, and refurbished unit guarantees.

How often should I get my AC washed or serviced?
For most homes we recommend a normal wash every few months during summer, and a full jet wash once a year. Homes with pets, dust, or heavy daily use may benefit from a cover wash every two months.
What is the difference between Normal Wash, Cover Wash, and Full Jet Wash?
A Normal Wash cleans filters, the evaporator coil surface, and the blower. A Cover Wash also removes the front louvers and cleans the drain tray for deeper sanitization. A Full Jet Wash uses a high-pressure pump and a waterproof catch-jacket to clean the coil and outdoor unit thoroughly, with no mess on your walls.
Do you provide doorstep second-hand AC inspections before buying?
Yes. Every used unit goes through a rigorous 30-point compressor, coil, and PCB inspection, and you receive a cooling performance report before you pay.
How quickly can a technician visit my location?
Same-day service is available across our service areas. Emergency breakdowns are handled 24/7, subject to technician availability.
Do second-hand ACs come with any warranty or testing?
Every refurbished unit comes with a 6 Months Testing Guarantee, backed by live coil testing and a complete cooling performance report.
What AC brands do you service?
We work on all major brands, including Daikin, Voltas, LG, Blue Star, Carrier, and Hitachi, using 100% genuine spares.
